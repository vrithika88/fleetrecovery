Failure Propagation Analysis

Understand the trigger → dependency → overload → future-damage chain.

How a failure propagates

A robot failure is not isolated: unfinished jobs, shared resources and dependency edges can push work onto healthy units. Extra load raises queue time and resource contention; if a stressed unit crosses its health envelope, it can become the next failure point.

01 · TriggerBattery, thermal, sensor, actuator or communication anomaly reduces usable capacity.
02 · DependencyJobs or resources waiting on the failed unit cannot complete on their original path.
03 · OverloadBackup robots inherit work, increasing load, queue pressure and recovery time.
04 · Future damageRepeated overload can lower efficiency, delay missions and create secondary failures.
